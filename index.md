models: a control-theoretic approach]({{ '/assets/thesis.pdf' | relative_url }})*,

[Jean-Baptiste Pomet](http://www-sop.inria.fr/members/Jean-Baptiste.Pomet/),
[Dario Prandi](https://dprn.github.io/) and
[Ludovic Sacchelli](https://sacchelli.github.io/).
